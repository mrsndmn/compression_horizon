Progressive Cramming: Reliable Token Compression and What It Reveals

One embedding reconstructs >1000 tokens — yet perfect reconstruction does not preserve useful semantics.

1 Progressive Cramming

Train one embedding for a frozen LM. Grow the target one token at a time; advance only after perfect reconstruction.

  • 100% greedy reconstruction at every accepted stage.
  • Stop at first failure; save the embedding trajectory.

5 Reconstruction ≠ Understanding

6 Attention Knockout
Llama-3.1-8B. Masking early layers restores downstream accuracy; masking late layers does not.

Early layers drive the disruption. The same pattern holds for Pythia-1.4B and SmolLM2-1.7B.

✓ Takeaways & Next Steps
  • Reconstruction ≠ semantics. Perfect decoding can coexist with downstream collapse.
  • Optimization headroom. Low-dim projection improves capacity; useful semantic compression remains open.
